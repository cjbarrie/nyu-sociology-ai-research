# Bounded scoping search

Date: 8 September 2026. Purpose: select readable examples for a faculty demonstration, not estimate an exhaustive literature or an effect size.

## Question and inclusion rule
AI chatbot use and subsequent support-seeking from friends/family. Include primary empirical studies of human relationships, social support, or adjacent outcomes useful for defining the target. Record indirectness; do not pool unlike outcomes. Search engine discovery followed by publisher, author, institutional, and arXiv retrieval. No subscription-database search was conducted.

## Screening decisions
Retained Fang, Guingrich, Chandra, De Freitas, Meng & Dai, Meng et al., Young, and Skjuve for core or mechanism context. Ueda retained only as a scope-boundary demonstration (professional support). No retained study is treated as a direct causal estimate of the precise informal-outreach outcome.

ACM 10.1145/3579505: discovery hit; full source retrieval failed; not included in the nine-record evidence table. Stumbling Into AI Emotional Dependence (arXiv:2606.04150): commentary/review, not an independent primary study; not counted as new empirical evidence. News, Reddit posts and clinical advice pages were excluded. Duplicate institutional/arXiv records were consolidated. Publisher restrictions were not bypassed.

## Access and version checks
Fang: reviewed v2 (2 October 2025); earlier v1 has different emphasis. Guingrich: reviewed v3 (13 October 2025). De Freitas: author-hosted published PDF, advance access 2025; issue is 2026. Meng & Dai and Skjuve: institutional abstracts/metadata only after unsuccessful PDF/redirect access. Young: manuscript reviewed; publication venue status left unverified. Full access details are in evidence.csv.

## Limits
This purposive, bounded collection cannot establish that no directly relevant studies exist. Search ranking, access constraints and English-language queries limit coverage. Exact behavioral measures and plausible counterfactuals require a more comprehensive review before a substantive manuscript.
