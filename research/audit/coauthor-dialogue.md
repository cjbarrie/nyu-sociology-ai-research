# Working through an argument with a skeptical coauthor

Scripted teaching example. These researcher turns are illustrative, not a transcript of Christopher's statements. The source and methods reports linked separately are actual agent reviews.

RESEARCHER: I think chatbots may displace friends because they are always available and easier to approach. The finding about heavier users seems consistent with that.

AI: It is consistent with that mechanism. But people who already have less support might also use the chatbot more. What would count as displacement in your argument: fewer requests, fewer responses, or weaker ties?

RESEARCHER: Fewer requests for help with a personal problem. I am not claiming that fewer requests always mean people are worse off.

AI: Then the loneliness findings do not measure your outcome. And the Fang study randomized modes and topics, not minutes of use. We can retain your mechanism as a hypothesis. We need evidence on outreach itself to evaluate it.

RESEARCHER: Could we ask people what they did after they needed support, and compare the proportion of needs that led to a friend?

AI: That would be useful descriptively, but there is another issue. If the chatbot changes what people experience or report as a need, the denominator changes. A lower proportion might reflect a different set of episodes. I would make total requests over a fixed follow-up the primary outcome and keep episode sequences as secondary measures.

RESEARCHER: I still want to know if the chatbot sometimes helps people reach out. Could we vary whether it suggests contacting a friend?

AI: Yes. That comparison would estimate the effect of the suggestion feature, relative to the standard chatbot offer. It would not tell us the overall effect of chatbot availability. We can include a usual-access comparison, but because people can use other services, we should describe the main contrast as an effect of offering this chatbot.

RESEARCHER: Let's write that narrower study. Leave the population and the smallest effect worth detecting as questions for us to resolve.

AI: I will revise the outcome and estimands, keep non-users in their assigned groups, and separate missing diaries from zero requests. The protocol will mark the unresolved choices. I will also ask the source reviewer to check that its introduction does not present the motivating association as a causal result.
