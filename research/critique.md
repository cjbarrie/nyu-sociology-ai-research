# Claim audit

## Behavioral model
The substitution-only decline follows from the rule removing requests; it is not evidence of real-world displacement. Bridging can reverse it, but its strength is assumed. The fixed network determines recipients and availability; it does not generate the sign of the request effect. Agents do not learn, friendships do not decay, and responders have unlimited capacity within a period. A response is not an effective resolution.

## Source inference
Fang v2 randomizes mode/topic, not total voluntary use. Guingrich v3 separates impact magnitude and direction; neither is observed informal outreach. Loneliness, preferences, perceived support, and professional-help attitudes are not interchangeable with contacting a friend. The nine-record collection is purposive and not exhaustive.

## Retrieval audit
The original recorded command received an empty HTTP 200 body for Ueda. This is not a successful document retrieval. The manifest now marks it invalid; the source was read through the web tool. The original recording is retained unchanged. Meng 2025 was similarly read through the web tool after the direct command received a 403.

## Corrected claim
Under the supplied substitution rule, requests decline. Sufficient bridging can reverse the result. Neither identifies the effect of actual chatbot use. Need incidence and actual outreach should be measured separately in a human study.
